What this is

A personal note-taking assistant

Thoughts Bot is a Telegram bot built for a single named user. Sent a voice memo or a line of text, it transcribes, tags, and files the note, then makes it searchable later by meaning, not just keywords. When a note describes a meeting or a call with a date and time, the bot can add it to that user’s Google Calendar.

It is not a public product, does not accept sign-ups, and is not offered or marketed to anyone beyond its one operator.

Privacy policy

What the calendar connection touches

Last updated 2026-09-05.

When a note describes a meeting, the text of that one note — a title, a date and time, and a short description drawn from the note itself — is sent to the Google Calendar API to create a single event. That is the entire extent of what leaves the user’s own server for this purpose.

  • No other Google data is requested. The connected scope covers only events this app creates, edits, or deletes — not mail, contacts, files, or any other calendar the account can see.
  • Nothing is sold, shared, or shown to anyone else. There are no advertising partners, analytics vendors, or data brokers involved anywhere in this app.
  • Nothing is retained beyond the event itself. The app keeps no separate copy, log, or backup of calendar data once an event is created; the note it came from stays only on the user’s own private server.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the user’s Google Account permissions page, which immediately stops the app from reaching the calendar.

Questions about this policy, or a request to delete anything created by this app, can be sent to steshenkostas@gmail.com.
